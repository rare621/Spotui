USpot is a music player that gets to know what you love. Find songs, keep your favorites together, and make your own radio stations. 🎧

**Android 8.0 or newer**

## 🚀 Start listening

1. **Download USpot** using the button above and open the file to install it.
2. **Sign in to Spotify** to bring in your music and playlists.
3. **Choose YouTube Music or Deezer** and confirm your audio connection.
4. **Complete SpotiFLAC verification**, then start listening. You can go Back during setup without losing completed connections.

Need to reconnect? Open **Settings → Connections & setup**. USpot also checks your connections when you open the app; a temporary network problem won't erase your saved accounts.

Already have USpot? Install the update over your current app. **Don't uninstall it** if you want to keep your history and settings. You can also save a backup in Settings.

Next time there's an update, USpot can let you know. Choose **Install** or **Cancel**, or check anytime in **Settings → Updates**.

## 💚 A fresh feel, wherever you listen

Enjoy a cleaner Library, tidier Radio pages and a refreshed Connect menu for your phone, speakers and headphones. Tap **USpot Premium** beneath your name in Settings to see the perks included with USpot. Pin favorite collections in Library, manage downloads and retry unfinished ones, and find what you want faster with grouped search results. The computer interface gets the same attention, too.

## ✨ Make yourself at home

- **🏠 A Home that learns your taste.** Familiar music to start, more of your favorites as you listen.
- **📌 Keep favorites close.** Pin playlists, albums and USpot radios in Library.
- **📚 Your music, together.** Liked Songs, playlists, albums, downloads and saved radios.
- **➕ Playlists your way.** Create a playlist, pick songs from your likes and other playlists, or discover something new. Use **Edit** to add and remove songs from playlists you own.
- **⋮ More from every song.** Tap the three dots or hold a song for its menu.
- **📻 USpot Radio.** Start a station from one song and let the music keep going.
- **🎛️ Radio Mix.** Blend up to five songs into one station. Choose **Stay close**, **Balanced** or **Explore more** to set the mood.
- **🔀 A little surprise.** Smart Shuffle adds relevant discoveries to your playlist listening.
- **👥 Following.** Catch new releases and popular songs from artists you follow.
- **📊 Your listening story.** See favorite songs, artists and recent plays in Stats and History.
- **⬇️ Take favorites offline.** Download music yourself, or turn on Smart Downloads in Settings. It's off until you choose to enable it.
- **🎚️ Your sound.** Equalizer presets and manual controls, plus a device menu for connected speakers and headphones.
- **🌗 Your look.** Light, dark, or follow your phone's theme.
- **🎧 Another way to keep listening.** Automatic playback and download backup tries another lookup when the usual one fails.
- **⏭️ A head start on the next song.** USpot starts preparing the next queued song after about two seconds of playback.
- **🚘 On the road.** Browse your music and recent radios with Android Auto.
- **🧹 Keep storage tidy.** Temporary music and artwork use a 256 MB managed cache budget, with automatic cleanup and a Storage page. Your listening data stays separate.
- **💾 Keep your memories.** Export and import your local data from Settings.

## 🖥️ Listen at your desk

<p align="center"><img src="assets/screenshots/desktop-browser.png" width="100%" alt="USpot on a computer" /></p>

Open **Settings → Desktop & Browser** on your phone and start desktop mode. Type the address it shows into your computer's browser. Keep your phone and computer on the same trusted Wi-Fi or router.

Your computer plays the music, and your listening adds to the phone app's history. Keep your phone connected while you listen. Stop desktop mode when you're finished. This feature is still being improved.

## 🔒 Your listening stays yours

USpot keeps its listening history on your device. It uses that history to personalize USpot, without sending your plays to Spotify to change Spotify's recommendations.
