Your phone is the connection.

Keep USpot desktop mode running on your phone and leave both devices on the same router. The computer may use Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

Locking the phone is fine. If it disconnects when idle, open USpot's app settings on Android and choose Battery → Unrestricted / Don't optimize. Keep the phone plugged in for long sessions.

Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs and router client isolation can prevent a connection. If the phone changes networks, restart desktop mode and use its new address.

Audio is played by this browser. The browser queue is separate from the phone queue. Some audio formats may not be supported by your browser. Anyone on your Wi-Fi can connect while desktop mode is running. Stop desktop mode when finished.
